Why are they being abandoned?

Most of the time owners give up their dogs due to costing too much, unexpected vet bills, and too much responsibility. A lot of owners tend to give up their pets when they tend to come across their pet getting sick and vet bills being too expensive that they cannot financially support the pet anymore. Other owners besides vet bills could be that they cannot be responsible for the pet and do not care about them anymore and end up giving them up. In extreme terms some pet owners get their pet for the sole purpose of breeding or testing for big company products and end up throwing them aside when they are no longer needed.
